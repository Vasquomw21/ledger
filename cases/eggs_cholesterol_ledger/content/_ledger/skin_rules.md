# Subject-specific writing rules (the "skin")

The universal rules — the bored reader test, paragraph architecture, no self-narration,
mechanism-over-apparatus, source discipline, voice, and the hard gates G0–G4 — live in the
shared `skill-ledger-write` assistant. **This file holds only the rules specific to THIS
subject.**

`skill-ledger-bootstrap` drafts a first version from your subject and your named reader; you
prune and sharpen it, and it grows as new failure modes surface.

Worked example — the Quantum KE white paper's pack:
- **W1** dual-audience register (a policy so-what AND scientific defensibility in every section)
- **W2** no-hype ladder (label every claim demonstrated / plausible / aspirational)
- **W3** primary-source-for-policy (legislation / official report / paper, never news)
- **W4** honest-on-region (surface genuine touchpoints; never manufacture a national narrative)
- **W5** progressive disclosure (plain main line; technical depth in callouts / appendices)

---

Subject rules — eggs / dietary cholesterol and CVD:
- **E1** observational-only: every claim here is from a cohort or meta-analysis, not a trial —
  never write "causes" or "reduces"; write "associated with" / "no association", and flag that
  the whole corpus shares confounding structure.
- **E2** distinguish overall from subgroup: an overall null and a diabetic-subgroup harm are
  different findings — keep them as separate claims with a `Qualifies` edge, never merge.
- **E3** performed vs settled: a confident meta-analysis (Drouin) *performs* settling; the
  contested positive cohort (Zhong) shows it is not consensus-settled — mark the distinction.
- **E4** correlated, not independent: meta-analyses pooling overlapping cohorts are not
  independent confirmations — record `Correlated-with` so the double-count is visible.
- **E5** name the decisive missing evidence: the question turns on confounding, which only an
  RCT could break — keep the absence of trial evidence in Known gaps, loudly.
