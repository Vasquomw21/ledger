# Subject-specific writing rules (the "skin")

The universal rules — the bored reader test, paragraph architecture, no self-narration,
mechanism-over-apparatus, source discipline, voice, and the hard gates G0–G4 — live in the
shared `skill-ledger-write` assistant. **This file holds only the rules specific to THIS
subject.**

`skill-ledger-bootstrap` drafts a first version from your subject and your named reader; you
prune and sharpen it, and it grows as new failure modes surface.

Worked example — the Quantum KE white paper's pack:
- **W1** dual-audience register (a policy so-what AND scientific defensibility in every section)
- **W2** no-hype ladder (label every claim demonstrated / plausible / aspirational)
- **W3** primary-source-for-policy (legislation / official report / paper, never news)
- **W4** honest-on-region (surface genuine touchpoints; never manufacture a national narrative)
- **W5** progressive disclosure (plain main line; technical depth in callouts / appendices)

---

Subject rules — LHC collision safety:
- **L1** distinguish *the bound* from *the conclusion*: a safety claim conditional on specific
  accretion / TeV-gravity models is not the same as a categorical "no risk" — flag the gap
  (this is what the rhetorical assessment on Giddings' "no risk whatsoever" records).
- **L2** map the disjunction honestly: the safety case rests on *either* Hawking evaporation
  *or* the astrophysical accretion bound — keep both branches visible, never collapse to one.
- **L3** settled vs performed: the LSAG review *performs* the settling; mark it `Status:
  performed-settling`, and reserve the crux for the load-bearing astrophysical argument.
- **L4** symmetric to the risk side: represent the risk-raising position (Plaga) as a
  quote-backed node and its rebuttal, not as a strawman — the reader must see the exchange.
- **L5** primary derivations for physics claims: ground the evaporation/accretion physics in
  the papers that derive it; where only the review asserts it, say so and log a Known gap.
