Calibration — confidence in the zoonotic-market conclusion

A confidence note on the conclusion that the early evidence locates emergence at the
market (and so weighs against a laboratory origin). The magnitudes are not
endorsed by any gate — only the discount shape is checked (`calibrated ≤
inside_view`); this records the reasoning, it does not certify the number.

  • Inside view (0.9). Taken at face value, the spatial

(Worobey 2022 #market-early-epicenter) and molecular
(Pekar 2022 #multiple-introductions) analyses are individually strong and mutually
reinforcing.

  • Out-of-model discount (0.1). Neither analysis models the **ascertainment

bias** in early case-finding (testing was itself centred on the market). The
signal is partly conditioned on where one looked — surfaced as a Known gap and as
the recorded faithfulness dispute.

  • Adversarial discount (0.1). Worobey and Pekar rest on the **same early-case

ascertainment**; treating them as independent over-counts the evidence (the
derived double-count finding). The two together are not 2× one.

  • Calibrated (0.7). Still the better-supported reading, but held below the

inside view to reflect the shared-data correlation and the unmodelled bias.
