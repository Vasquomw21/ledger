This case was assembled to test out-of-domain generalisability across quantum advantage, genomics,

Overview

Review-question: Which sources separate demonstrated quantum advantage, erased/caveated QML speedups, genomics relevance, and cryptographic threat?
Inclusion-criteria: Sources must anchor a declared position in the quantum/genomics inquiry, supply a canonical algorithm/result/rebuttal/standard, and have stamped verified-claim ledgers.
Exclusion-criteria: Sources that do not bear on a declared position, only duplicate an already captured claim, or lack accessible quotable text are excluded or listed as gaps.
Update-procedure: Add a search row and included/excluded/unavailable entry for every corpus change; update semantic health when new benchmarks or standards alter a gap or position.
