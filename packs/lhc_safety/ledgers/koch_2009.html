Verified Claims — Koch, Bleicher & Stöcker (2009)

A second exclusion of the LHC black-hole disaster scenario, ingested to close the Known gap on
independent safety analyses: does the mainstream-safe position rest on more than the LSAG /
Giddings analysis? Koch et al. present their argument as independent — but explicitly quote
Giddings' conclusion and draw on the same astrophysical bodies (white dwarfs / neutron stars),
which makes the degree of independence an open assessment question rather than a given.
