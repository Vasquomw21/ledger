Source flow

Source Flow

This case was assembled to stress-test overlapping cohorts and conflicting meta-analytic claims, not
to reproduce a full nutrition systematic review. The flow records the route each included source took
into the corpus; it does not claim the search is complete.

Overview

Review-question: How do egg and dietary-cholesterol sources differ once overlapping cohorts and outcome scope are made explicit?
Inclusion-criteria: Sources must contribute a meta-analysis, primary cohort, subgroup result, or cohort-overlap control relevant to eggs/cholesterol and cardiovascular outcomes, and have stamped verified-claim ledgers.
Exclusion-criteria: Sources that do not address egg/cholesterol exposure, cardiovascular/mortality outcomes, cohort overlap, or a declared position are excluded or listed as gaps.
Update-procedure: Add a search row and included/excluded/unavailable entry for each corpus change; update risk-of-bias/certainty notes when domain templates are added.
Last-updated: 2026-06-15
