Source quality note. The only available copy (the one the brief links) is a 1946 scanned
typescript with a poor OCR text layer. Per the quote-first rule, the quotes below are exactly the
spans that verify verbatim against the extracted text; where OCR mangled the surrounding words the

desirable" in the original, but those final words are OCR-degraded in this scan, so the
verifiable quote ends at "on the". This is the honesty boundary working as designed: form is
guaranteed, degraded text is not silently repaired.
