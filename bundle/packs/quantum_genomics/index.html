Processes — how it is built

Each source travels one pipeline: check (already on disk?) → fetch → verify (every quote proved byte-for-byte against the fetched source) → stamp (hashes bound) → structure (typed edges resolve cross-ledger) → assess (human judgement, sealed). The verify step runs where the corpus lives — in the project, at every commit.

That pipeline is one instrument in a five-layer integrity stack — the distinct ways knowledge work fails, ordered foundation → delivery, crossed with how strongly Ledger catches a failure in each. Enforcement strength falls as you climb: the foundation is a mechanical guarantee, the top is disciplined judgement. This is the map behind the guaranteed-vs-judged boundary.

Integrity layerFailure it targetsHow strongly it is caught
1 · Fidelityfabricated quote · wrong paper · quote out of contextGuarantee Fabrication is blocked mechanically; wrong-paper is structural; out-of-context use is measured + assisted (a detection rate on a labelled benchmark).
2 · Completenessselection bias · missing sourceStructural Structural + assisted — a floor against a silent empty position, not a representativeness proof.
3 · Coherencebroken or absent links · contradiction · stale claimStructural Structural (every edge resolves + is grounded) plus an assisted semantic pass for meaning.
4 · Validitythe conclusion doesn't follow · cherry-pickingJudged Judged — ledger analyze assists (load-bearing claims, dependency closure, double-counts). This is the frontier.
5 · Clarityopacity · undefined jargonJudged Judged — enforced in the writing loop by the bored-reader test.
